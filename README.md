### Who I am

**Electronics engineer** based in **Bengaluru, India** — autodidact, overthinker, lifelong learner.  
By day I work at the intersection of **VLSI CAD**, **physical verification**, and **layout automation** ([Marvell Technology](https://www.linkedin.com/in/giridhar-salana)). By night (and in commits) I ship **Rust**, **Python**, **Flutter**, and **TypeScript** side projects — from **GDSII parsers** to **LLM-powered CLIs** and **mobile apps**.

> *Everything is an experiment.* — also how I treat new tools, flows, and repos.
